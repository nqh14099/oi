PART 01 · DRIVERS OF CHANGE

Three Forces for Reform

Economic Change

A market economy needs institutions that are responsive, predictable, and able to coordinate complex decisions.

Administrative Efficiency

Overlapping duties and unclear accountability can slow decisions and make procedures harder to navigate.

Global Integration

International cooperation and investment benefit from transparent rules, consistent enforcement, and reliable services.
