## Scene 1: REFORMING VIETNAM’S POLITICAL SYSTEM

Good morning. Our topic is reforming Vietnam’s political system. We will use practical examples to explain why reform is necessary.

### Beat 2: Our question

Our central question is simple: how can reform make Vietnam’s political system work better for its people? We will connect historical context, current changes and our responsibilities as students.

## Scene 2: A system that serves people

Planned time: 40 seconds. Speaker: __________

### Beat 1: Leadership

The textbook explains that the Party leads the political system and is also part of it. This is our starting point.

### Beat 2: Responsibilities

The State manages public affairs under the law. The Vietnam Fatherland Front and socio-political organisations also have distinct roles. These roles should remain clear when we discuss reform.

### Beat 3: People

The people exercise their right to mastery. Reform therefore concerns how institutions organise work, coordinate and take responsibility. What happens when these connections become unclear?

Source notes: TEXTBOOK, PLATFORM2011

## Scene 3: Why reform becomes necessary

Planned time: 140 seconds. Speaker: __________

### Beat 1: Enter

Imagine a person trying to submit one application. They see several offices and several possible routes. They need to know where to begin. This scene is an illustration of a problem, not a record of a real application.

### Beat 2: Tangle

The red cords represent overlapping procedures, crossing responsibilities and unclear coordination. They do not mean that the whole political system is chaotic. Notice the blocked route. If nobody takes the lead, the citizen may be asked to return or repeat information.

### Beat 3: Apply Reform

Let us apply reform. The cords separate and the routes become clearer. This shows the intended direction: clearer tasks and better coordination. The movement is a visual explanation. It is not evidence that a reform has already solved every problem.

### Beat 4: Economic change

Why is reform necessary? First, economic and social development creates new demands. Institutions need the capacity to respond. A structure that once worked may need to change as public needs and economic activity become more complex.

### Beat 5: Effectiveness

Second, administrative work needs clear responsibility. Resolution 18 calls for one agency to take the main responsibility for each task, while others coordinate. Fewer offices alone do not tell us whether an application becomes easier to complete. A clear lead also gives staff and citizens a place to ask for updates. Coordination remains necessary; the other offices still have duties.

### Beat 6: Integration

Third, deeper international integration raises demands for capable and predictable institutions. These pressures make reform an ongoing task. After the pathways change, we still need to ask: can staff carry out their duties, and can people use the service?

Source notes: NQ18, WB2045

## Scene 4: Change has a history

Planned time: 50 seconds. Speaker: __________

### Beat 1: 1986

In 1986, the Sixth Congress responded to crisis with comprehensive Đổi Mới, or Renovation. It renewed economic thinking while keeping the socialist goal.

### Beat 2: 2017

The process continued through Party renewal in 1992 and the 2011 Platform. In 2017, Resolution 18 focused on organisational structure, overlapping functions and responsibility.

### Beat 3: 2025

In 2025, Resolution 60 provided direction for a specific local-government change. These milestones have different roles. They are not identical events or a simple chain that proves later reforms successful.

Source notes: TEXTBOOK, NQ18, NQ60

Optional historical bridge for Q&A: The Third Plenum of the Seventh Central Committee in June 1992 addressed Party renewal and rectification (textbook pp. 281–282). The 2011 Platform further clarified the political system and leadership methods (pp. 346–348). Do not read every document name in the main talk. Đại hội VI: pp. 260–264.

## Scene 5: 63 → 34

Planned time: 110 seconds. Speaker: __________

### Beat 1: Before

Here is our main practical example. The model begins with 63 provincial-level units. Its shapes are stylised, so they should not be read as official administrative boundaries.

### Beat 2: After

The move to 34 units is a verified organisational change. The policy aims to reduce administrative focal points and improve coordination. The animation makes the scale visible; it does not measure the quality of public services.

### Beat 3: Two tiers

The Party Central Committee adopted Resolution 60 in April 2025. The National Assembly then adopted Resolution 202 and Law 72 in June. New local authorities began operating on 1 July, with province and commune levels. The district level ended. Commune-level units include communes, wards and special zones.

### Beat 4: Success

This gives us three layers: Party direction, a legal framework and implementation. Success depends on capable staff, clear duties, accountability and people’s experience. If citizens still submit the same papers in several places, changing the number of units is not enough. We need evidence about the actual service. A useful assessment asks about repeated submissions, access to help and clarity of responsibility, rather than relying on the new unit count alone.

Source notes: NQ60, NQ202, LAW72, TEXTBOOK, WB2045

Reference detail: Resolution 60, 12 April 2025 → Resolution 202, 12 June 2025, and Law 72, 16 June 2025 → new local authorities operating 1 July 2025. This simplified sequence does not list every implementing instrument. National Assembly and Government are different institutions.

## Scene 7: The journey continues

Our citizen returns. The first question is practical: where do I submit? An online portal helps only if people can use it. Hands-on support belongs inside the same service journey. The person who cannot use the portal should still have a practical route to complete the task.

### Beat 2: Process

## Scene 11: Đổi Mới 1986

Planned time: 75 seconds. Speaker: __________

### Beat 1: Choose

Read the question and allow time to think. Ask the class to raise their hands for A, B, C or D in turn. Select the class’s answer, then press Submit. Explain the reasoning briefly. Do not advance until the discussion is finished.

By the mid-1980s, Vietnam was in a socio-economic crisis under a centrally planned, subsidised economy. Which decision best matches the Party’s response at the Sixth National Congress in 1986?

- **A.** Tighten central planning and subsidies to restore control over production.
- **B.** Replace the socialist goal with a market model adopted from other countries.
- **C.** Postpone economic changes until political institutions had been reorganised first.
- **D.** Renew economic thinking and end the bureaucratic subsidy system, keeping the socialist goal.

Correct answer: **D**

The Sixth Congress, held in Hanoi on 15–18 December 1986, set out the line of comprehensive Đổi Mới. It called for renewing thinking, starting with economic thinking, and for ending the centrally planned, bureaucratic subsidy mechanism, and it is seen as a turning point in Vietnam’s transition to socialism. This is the starting point for later reforms of the political system; it does not show that any particular later reform has succeeded.

Source notes: TEXTBOOK

Optional short oral follow-up: What would you check to know whether public services have improved? Take one brief contribution. “Starting point” refers to Đổi Mới within this presentation, not the beginning of Vietnam’s political system.

Which description matches the local government model that began operating on 1 July 2025?

- **A.** 34 provincial-level units, with province and commune tiers.
- **B.** 63 provincial-level units, with province and commune tiers.
- **C.** 34 provincial-level units, with province, district, and commune tiers.
- **D.** 63 provincial-level units, with province, district, and commune tiers.

Correct answer: **A**

From 1 July 2025, local government has two tiers: province-level units (provinces and centrally run cities) and commune-level units (communes, wards and special zones), and the district level has ended. The model follows Party Central Committee Resolution 60-NQ/TW of 12 April 2025, and the National Assembly adopted Resolution 202/2025/QH15 on province-level units on 12 June 2025. This is an organizational change; whether services improve needs separate evidence.

## Scene 13: People at the center

Planned time: 75 seconds. Speaker: __________

### Beat 1: Choose

Read the question and allow time to think. Ask the class to raise their hands for A, B, C or D in turn. Select the class’s answer, then press Submit. Explain the reasoning briefly. Do not advance until the discussion is finished.

The Sixth Congress (1986) put “take the people as the root” first among its main lessons. A commune offers online public services, but many older residents cannot complete the forms. Which action best applies this lesson?

- **A.** Count the online applications received and treat the total as the measure of success.
- **B.** Publish a written guide on the portal and ask residents to try again alone.
- **C.** Provide hands-on help with submitting and show residents how to track progress.
- **D.** Keep the online process unchanged and refer residents to relatives for help.

Correct answer: **C**

The Sixth Congress placed taking the people as the root first among its four main lessons, together with building and promoting the working people’s mastery. For public services, this means judging reform by whether residents can actually use and follow them. Hands-on help and progress tracking address the barrier directly; application counts, a written guide or a family referral do not show that it has been removed. A policy calling for such support does not establish that every resident has already received it.

Source notes: TEXTBOOK, HANOI12

Historical reference: textbook p. 262 (the first lesson of the Sixth Congress), p. 264 (the people’s mastery), pp. 405–406 (later synthesis). Applying this to online services is the group’s interpretation, not a claim about technology discussed in 1986.

## Scene 14: One task, one lead

Planned time: 75 seconds. Speaker: __________

### Beat 1: Choose

Read the question and allow time to think. Ask the class to raise their hands for A, B, C or D in turn. Select the class’s answer, then press Submit. Explain the reasoning briefly. Do not advance until the discussion is finished.

Resolution 18-NQ/TW (2017) sets the principle that an agency may handle many tasks, but each task should have one lead agency with main responsibility. Three offices now review the same permit application, and each waits for the others. Which arrangement best follows this principle?

- **A.** Keep the three reviews running in parallel and combine the opinions at the end.
- **B.** Name one office as lead with main responsibility, and have the others coordinate through it.
- **C.** Merge the three offices into one but keep the same three review steps.
- **D.** Ask the applicant to coordinate between the offices and report any conflicts.

Correct answer: **B**

Resolution 18-NQ/TW (25 October 2017) of the 12th Central Committee calls for a political system that is streamlined and works effectively and efficiently; it names overlapping functions and weak accountability among the problems to solve. Merging offices, running reviews in parallel or passing coordination to citizens does not make responsibility clear. The example is illustrative and does not show how far overlap has been reduced in practice.

## Q&A reserve

**What is the Party’s role in reform?** The Party sets major orientations and leads through policy, organisation, personnel, inspection and supervision. It leads the political system and is also part of it. State institutions turn orientations into legal rules and carry out their duties under law. See PLATFORM2011 and textbook pp. 347–348.

**Does Đổi Mới change the socialist goal?** No. The Sixth Congress renewed thinking and the management mechanism while retaining the socialist goal. Later reforms have their own historical contexts. See textbook pp. 260–264.

**Does one lead agency remove the duties of other agencies?** No. One agency takes main coordinating responsibility. Others still perform their duties and cooperate within their authority. See NQ18.

**Does “no exceptions” describe every actual case?** It is a policy principle. Consistent implementation needs evidence. See REPORT14.

**How will the group apply these lessons?** We should check original sources, divide tasks, review each other’s work and explain what evidence can and cannot show. Use past tense only after members confirm work actually completed.

- Cương lĩnh 2011: Platform for National Construction in the Period of Transition to Socialism (supplemented and developed in 2011).
- Đại hội XIV: 14th National Party Congress.
- Quốc hội: National Assembly; Chính phủ: Government.
- Chính quyền địa phương hai cấp: two-tier local government.
- Trách nhiệm chính: main responsibility; trách nhiệm giải trình: accountability.
